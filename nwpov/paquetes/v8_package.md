## B. Numbers check
- Aumento: ~12,000 / año brutos -> ~800 / mes netos (ilustrativo, "about").
- Reparto: 600 / mes a inversion automatica + 200 / mes a gasto de disfrute = 800.
- FV de 600 / mes, 10 años (n = 120), 6% anual (0.5% / mes): 600 x ((1.005^120 - 1) / 0.005) = 600 x 163.88 = ~98,300 -> "roughly ninety-eight thousand".
- Vecino: 800 / mes en pagos nuevos x 12 x 5 años = 48,000 -> "forty-eight thousand dollars in five years".
- Recorte de ingreso (año 6): 800 / mes = lo mismo que el aumento; Sam pausa la inversion unos meses, sin cambiar su gasto de vida. En la narracion se dice que "the picture stays similar" (no se da cifra).

**Descripcion:**
An email arrives with the raise you have waited two years for, and you quietly finish your coffee in the same chipped blue mug. Your neighbor gets a raise the same month and trades up to a bigger black SUV. This is a calm story about two very different decisions.
